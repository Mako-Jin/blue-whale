   - 新增 `common/lock/annotation/DistributedLock.java`：`prefix`/`biz`/`key`(SpEL)/`waitTime`/`leaseTime`/`failFast`
   - 新增 `common/lock/aop/DistributedLockAspect.java`：`@Around("@annotation(distributedLock)")`，`MethodBasedEvaluationContext` 解析 SpEL，Key = `{prefix}:{biz}:{值}`；`tryLock(waitTime, leaseTime, SECONDS)`；`finally` 内 `isLocked() && isHeldByCurrentThread()` 才 `unlock()`；失败按 `failFast` 抛 `BizException`；`@Order(HIGHEST_PRECEDENCE+100)` 使锁覆盖整个事务
   - 新增 `common/lock/config/DistributedLockAutoConfiguration.java`：`@ConditionalOnClass({RedissonClient, Aspect})` 条件装配
   - `common/core/exception/ErrorCode.java` 新增 `LOCK_ACQUIRE_FAILED(429)`
