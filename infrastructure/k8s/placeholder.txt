Kubernetes 部署文件占位

正式上线可按如下结构补充 (建议配合 Kustomize / Helm):

bluewhale/
├── namespace.yaml             (已创建)
├── configmap/
│   ├── nginx-conf.yaml
│   └── application-prod.yaml
├── secret/
│   └── app-secrets.yaml       (勿提交真实值, 使用 ExternalSealed/GitOps)
├── deployment/
│   ├── whale-gateway.yaml
│   ├── whale-auth.yaml
│   └── whale-frontend.yaml
├── service/
│   ├── whale-gateway.yaml
│   ├── whale-auth.yaml
│   └── whale-frontend.yaml
├── ingress/
│   └── bluewhale-ingress.yaml
├── statefulset/
│   ├── mysql.yaml
│   └── redis.yaml
├── pdb/                       PodDisruptionBudget
├── hpa/                       HorizontalPodAutoscaler
└── kustomization.yaml

基础设施 (bluewhale-infra namespace):
  mysql, redis, nacos, rabbitmq, prometheus, grafana, istio 等.
